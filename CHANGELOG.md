# Version 0.5.0

- One short hourly bell, sharing knob volume with the ticking. Zero mutes both.
- No startup bell, duplicate hour strikes or replay after time synchronization.
- Swipe to change clocks; hold the knob for the group menu, turn and press to select.
- Selected SD group caches in RAM; normal ticks and swipes do not access the card.
- Eight built-in faces and 34 additional declared-CC0 faces in four SD groups.
- Blue Meridian is included in every group. Face, group and volume persist.
- Oversized rotating layers use cheaper sampling to keep one-second updates.
- Installer verifies hashes and backs up the recipient's entire flash first.

Download the attached **orbOfManyFaces-0.5.0.zip** and follow its README. Hardware:
Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75, 16 MB flash / 8 MB PSRAM. Configure your own
Wi-Fi/timezone in Orb OS before installing. For all 42 designs, copy the included
SD packs to `/clock-groups/` or use `tools/upload_groups.py`.

Source code ZIP/TAR downloads generated by GitHub do not include installer binaries.
Artwork credits and license notices are included; community themes outside this
public bundle can have other terms.
